=== 🚀 Quick Start

https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3827497087
[olist]
[*] [b]Find bags[/b]: police stations have S.T.A.R.S. bags, military and medical sites have Umbrella bags. Police, soldier and medic zombies can also drop one when they die.
[*] [b]Sew your own[/b]: with enough Tailoring you can sew them from the Tailoring category of the crafting menu. No recipe to learn first.
[*] [b]Upgrade[/b]: take the bag off and use "Reinforce Bag to Tier 1/2/3", one tier at a time. Vanilla bags can be upgraded too. The first upgrade turns the bag into its "Reinforced" version: it weighs 0.1 and does not slow you down, and vanilla bags switch to the S.T.A.R.S. or Umbrella look.
[*] [b]Old bags[/b]: bags left over from Bag Upgrade Plus only let you take items out. Use "Convert Old Plus Bag to Reinforced" to turn one into the reinforced version of the same bag; items can stay inside.
[*] [b]Full bags on the floor[/b]: when the vanilla "Wear" option can't pick one up, right-click it and choose "Wear (with contents)".
[/olist]
[b]Server owners[/b]: if you are coming from Bag Upgrade Plus, read the "Bag Upgrade Plus Takeover: Server Switch" chapter first. Both mods must be swapped in the same restart.

[h3]Two looks[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]Same stats[/b]: S.T.A.R.S. and Umbrella differ only in colors and where you find them.
[*] [b]Custom models[/b]: all seven bags have their own models and inventory icons, with separate male and female models.
[/list]

[h3]What fits inside[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]Medic Hip Pouch[/b]: only items in the First Aid category, such as bandages and pills.
[*] [b]Ammo Bandolier[/b]: only loose rounds and shotgun shells. Boxes of ammo do not fit.
[*] [b]Ammo Bandolier reloading[/b]: wearing it makes reloading rounds, magazines and shells faster.
[*] [b]Scout Chest Rig[/b]: no single item heavier than 1.3 (a Claw Hammer, for example), like the vanilla Chest Rig.
[*] [b]Reinforced versions keep them[/b]: these three bags keep their restrictions and faster reloading after they become reinforced.
[*] [b]The other four[/b]: no restrictions.
[/list]

[h3]Bedroll attachment[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]Day Pack[/b]: a bedroll can hang under it, like the vanilla Hiking Backpack.
[*] [b]Expedition Frame Pack[/b]: a bedroll can hang under it, like the vanilla Big Hiking Backpack.
[/list]

[h3]Which bags can be upgraded[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]This mod's bags[/b]: all seven models in both looks, plus the swapped-side fanny pouches.
[*] [b]Vanilla bags[/b]: 80 wearable backpacks, satchels, fanny packs and chest rigs without item restrictions, such as the Small Backpack, Hiking Backpack, Military Backpack and Duffel Bag.
[*] [b]Vanilla containers that cannot[/b]: the restricted Bullets Bandoliers and the Shoulder Holster, plus key rings and wallets.
[*] [b]Old Bag Upgrade Plus bags[/b]: not through the upgrade recipes. Converting one gives the reinforced version at the highest tier.
[/list]

[h3]How much each tier adds[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]Full upgrade matches Bag Upgrade Plus[/b]: at the highest tier, capacity is 49 (50 minus the reinforced weight of 0.1) and Encumbrance Reduction is 100.
[*] [b]Even steps in between[/b]: each tier adds the same share of the way from the original values to full, rounded to whole numbers.
[*] [b]Examples[/b]: a vanilla Small Backpack goes 15/70 → 26/80 → 38/90 → 49/100; a vanilla Fanny Pack goes 1/85 → 17/90 → 33/95 → 49/100 (capacity / Encumbrance Reduction).
[*] [b]Hydration Packs[/b]: water capacity rises the same way up to 5 L, for example 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 L for the vanilla Hydration Pack and 3 → 3.7 → 4.3 → 5 L for the camo one.
[*] [b]Already higher[/b]: a bag whose Encumbrance Reduction is already above the full value keeps its own.
[*] [b]Server settings[/b]: the highest tier (0–3) and the full capacity and Encumbrance Reduction are sandbox options; see "Sandbox Options and Defaults".
[/list]
=== 🧰 Reinforced Bags
Every upgraded bag is a "Reinforced" bag: this mod's own item with "(Reinforced)" after its name. Reinforced vanilla bags keep their vanilla shape but switch to this mod's S.T.A.R.S. (navy) or Umbrella (olive) look, with the theme in front of the name, such as "S.T.A.R.S. Hiking Backpack (Reinforced)" or "Umbrella Duffel Bag (Reinforced)". This mod's seven bags keep their own look, such as "S.T.A.R.S. Day Pack (Reinforced)".
{{img:reinforced-tooltip-en.jpg}}

[h3]What is different from the original[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]Weighs 0.1[/b]: the bag itself is almost weightless, so every bag reaches 49 when fully upgraded.
[*] [b]No slowdown[/b]: wearing it does not reduce run or combat speed; only the weight of what is inside counts as usual.
[*] [b]Hydration Packs[/b]: water capacity reaches 5 L when fully upgraded; with water inside, the name shows as "Water (S.T.A.R.S. Reinforced Hydration Pack)".
[*] [b]Satchels[/b]: worn in a second satchel slot on the other side; see "Second Satchel Slot".
[*] [b]Military Backpacks, Duffel Bags, Hiking and Big Hiking Backpacks[/b]: get extra hotbar attachment slots; see "Reinforced Bag Attachment Slots".
[*] [b]School bags[/b]: the reinforced "Small Backpack (Reinforced)" (what all five vanilla school bags upgrade into) can carry a bedroll underneath, like Bag Upgrade Plus's school bag; the originals cannot.
[*] [b]Duffel bags[/b]: while you wear one, long guns, big blades and shovels on your back hang outside the bag instead of sinking into it, like Bag Upgrade Plus's duffel bags. As with vanilla backpacks, a guitar cannot go on your back then; one already there is taken off into your inventory when you put the bag on.
[*] [b]Everything else stays[/b]: capacity and Encumbrance Reduction follow the tier; item restrictions, bedroll attachments and fanny pack swapping work like the original.
[/list]

[h3]What carries over when a bag becomes reinforced[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]Contents[/b]: everything inside stays, including bags inside bags and liquids.
[*] [b]Your name[/b]: a bag you renamed keeps your name. Other bags take the reinforced name (dirty or worn prefixes do not carry over). Fanny packs swapped front or back earlier can be an exception; see "Known Limitations".
[*] [b]The rest[/b]: condition and favorite mark carry over. The look switches to the theme; reverting brings back the original bag and its original color.
[/list]

[h3]Which bags have a reinforced version[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]Vanilla bags are merged by shape[/b]: the 80 vanilla bags are grouped by shape (the model worn on the body) into 22 shapes, each with a S.T.A.R.S. and an Umbrella version: 44 in total. For example, all 22 duffel-style bags (police, sports, tool, medical and so on) upgrade into "Duffel Bag (Reinforced)".
[*] [b]Best stats of the group[/b]: starting capacity, Encumbrance Reduction and Hydration Pack water capacity are the highest in the shape, so no tier is lower than the original's; full upgrade is still 49/100.
[*] [b]Which theme you get[/b]: police, sheriff and SWAT bags become S.T.A.R.S.; military, ALICE, camo, chest rigs, gun cases, medical, crafted and natural materials (leather, hide, wicker, canvas, sheets), and green or khaki bags become Umbrella; other colors become S.T.A.R.S. Bags that come in several colors (such as the seven duffel bag colors) follow the color of your bag. For the other theme, use "Switch Reinforced Bag Theme" below.
[*] [b]This mod's bags[/b]: the seven bags in both looks and the swapped-side fanny pouches each have their own reinforced version: 18 in total.
[*] [b]Not in loot or on zombies[/b]: you only get them by upgrading, converting old bags or the recipes below.
[/list]

=== 🧰 Second Satchel Slot
{{img:satchel2-front-en.jpg}}
{{img:satchel2-back-en.jpg}}
[list]
[*] [b]Two satchels at once[/b]: a reinforced satchel is worn in a second slot on the other side, so you can wear it together with one normal satchel. The inventory panel shows both containers.
[*] [b]Which bags count as satchels[/b]: the 13 vanilla satchels (Satchel, Mail Satchel, Crafted Hide Satchel, crafted cloth satchels and others) and this mod's Courier Satchel. Their reinforced versions all go in this slot.
[*] [b]Look[/b]: it hangs behind the other hip, a little smaller than the original so it stays clear of the hanging arm, and the two straps cross on the chest and back. The badges on the bag are not mirrored.
[*] [b]One reinforced satchel at a time[/b]: two reinforced satchels both need the second slot, so they cannot be worn together.
[*] [b]Bag Upgrade Plus satchels[/b]: become reinforced satchels when converted and use the second slot too.

=== 🧰 Wearing Full Bags Off the Floor
When a bag on the floor is very full, the vanilla "Wear" option first moves it into your inventory and fails if it does not fit. In that case the right-click menu adds "Wear (with contents)".
{{img:floorwear-menu-en.jpg}}
[list]
[*] [b]How it works[/b]: your character walks to the bag and puts it on directly. Everything stays inside the bag the whole time; nothing spills onto the floor.
[*] [b]When it shows up[/b]: only when the vanilla "Wear" would fail. Empty or half-full bags use the vanilla option.
[*] [b]Which bags[/b]: any wearable bag, including vanilla bags and bags from other mods.
[*] [b]Time[/b]: about the time to pick up the bag and put each item back, so more and heavier items take longer. For example, 49 items weighing 1 each take about 50 seconds; Dextrous halves it, All Thumbs or clumsy gloves double it.
[*] [b]A bag already in that slot[/b]: it goes into your inventory if it fits, otherwise it drops at your feet with its contents.
[*] [b]Where you stand[/b]: the bag must be on the same floor, on a square you can reach without a wall in between.
[*] [b]Safehouses[/b]: you cannot take bags from someone else's safehouse.
[*] [b]Multiplayer[/b]: if two players grab the same bag, only one gets it; nothing is duplicated.
[/list]

=== 🧰 Bag Upgrade Plus Takeover
Bag Upgrade Plus (BUP below) has been removed from the Workshop. This mod takes over the bags in its saves: after switching, the old bags still load and everything inside them is still there.

[h3]Supported versions[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]Main version[/b]: Bag Upgrade Plus (3668094025, mod id BagUpgradePlusUpdated).
[*] [b]Realistic version[/b]: 3825931520, mod id BagUpgradePlusRealistic.
[*] [b]Older branches[/b]: 3403697073, 3640342538, 3780399230; all use mod id BagUpgradePlus.
[*] [b]CloudBag Upgrade Plus[/b]: 3811180026, mod id CloudBag. Same item names as the main version, same values as the Realistic version.
[*] [b]EFT compatibility version not supported[/b]: 3781184534 depends on another mod (EFTBP), so this mod does not take it over.
[/list]

[h3]What old bags look like after the switch[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]Look and weight[/b]: same as the matching vanilla bag. An old ALICE pack looks like the vanilla Military Backpack.
[*] [b]Capacity and Encumbrance Reduction[/b]: kept from the save. The main version saved 49/100; the Realistic version and CloudBag keep their own values, for example ALICE 40/90 and Satchel 18/80.
[*] [b]Take out only[/b]: by default nothing can be put back in, only taken out, and the server enforces it too. The sandbox option "Lock old Bag Upgrade Plus bags" turns this off.
[*] [b]No new copies[/b]: old bags never appear in loot, on zombies or as any recipe result.
[*] [b]Drinks and plushies[/b]: the drinks and plushies the original mod changed stay in your inventory or bags after the switch; they just turn back into vanilla items. They could never hang on Plus bags in the first place, so the switch does not make anything fall off or disappear.
[*] [b]Servers that define those slots[/b]: if your server also runs a mod that defines those attachment slots, anything hanging there should be detached into your inventory after the switch. This case has not been tested.
[*] [b]Bedrolls on old school bags[/b]: a bedroll hanging under an old Plus school bag is detached into your inventory after the switch, because the old bag now works like the vanilla school bag, which has no bedroll slot. Convert it to the reinforced version to hang the bedroll again.
[*] [b]Water bottles[/b]: BUP's two water bottles keep working with their water. They are neither locked nor convertible. The empty soda bottle that only the older branches 3403697073 and 3640342538 have is not taken over and disappears after the switch.
[/list]

[h3]Converting to a reinforced bag[/h3]
{{img:craft-convert-en.jpg}}
[list]
[*] [b]Recipe[/b]: "Convert Old Plus Bag to Reinforced" in the Tailoring category of the crafting menu.
[*] [b]Requirements[/b]: the old bag must not be worn; items can stay inside.
[*] [b]Materials[/b]: a Needle and 2 Thread. No Tailoring level needed.
[*] [b]Result[/b]: the reinforced version of the matching vanilla bag, already at the highest tier (tier 3, 49/100 by default). The theme follows the old bag's color, by the same rules as upgrading a vanilla bag. An old ALICE pack becomes "Umbrella Military Backpack (Reinforced)", an old fanny pack becomes "S.T.A.R.S. Fanny Pack (Reinforced)". Reverting it later gives the matching vanilla bag.
[*] [b]Carried over[/b]: contents, liquids and your custom name. The 5 L of water in an old Hydration Pack stays.
[*] [b]Satchels[/b]: after converting they go in the second satchel slot.
[/list]
=== 🧰 Bag Upgrade Plus Takeover: Server Switch
[b]Do it in a single restart[/b]: disabling BUP and enabling this mod must happen together. If the server runs once without BUP and you add this mod later, every old bag loaded during that time is deleted along with its contents.

[olist]
[*] [b]Back up[/b]: stop the server and back up the whole server save (world and player data).
[*] [b]Swap the mods[/b]: in the server config, remove every BUP from Mods= (mod ids BagUpgradePlusUpdated, BagUpgradePlusRealistic, BagUpgradePlus, CloudBag) and add MinidoracatBagsFor42. In WorkshopItems=, remove BUP's Workshop ID and add this mod's 3827497087.
[*] [b]Check after start[/b]: server-console.txt has no "WARNING: another Bag Upgrade Plus is loaded" line, and players who log in still have their old bags, take-out only.
[*] [b]If the server already ran without the original mod[/b]: bags loaded into the game during that time were deleted along with everything inside them. Switching to this mod cannot bring them back; only a backup from before that start can. The deleted bags are those worn or in the inventory of players who logged in during that time, plus bags on the ground or in containers near where anyone went. Bags worn or carried by players who did not log in during that time, and bags in places nobody visited, are still there and show up normally after switching to this mod.
[/olist]

[h3]What happens if BUP and this mod run together[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]Warning[/b]: the server's server-console.txt and each player's console.txt get one line "WARNING: another Bag Upgrade Plus is loaded; disable it."
[*] [b]Old bags are still there[/b]: but BUP overrides the definitions of some old bags (the two fanny packs in our tests, with both the main version and CloudBag). The active= part of the warning names the version.
[*] [b]Fix[/b]: disable BUP and keep only this mod.
[/list]

[h3]Single-player saves[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]Same rule, one switch[/b]: back up the save folder first. Pick the save on the Load screen, disable BUP and enable this mod in its mod list, then load. The old bags, everything inside them and the water bottles are all there; old bags are take-out only.
[/list]

=== ⚠️ Known Limitations
[list]
[*] [b]Living zombies never show the new bags[/b]: zombie bags come from their outfits, and a mod can only replace a whole outfit. So the bag is put into the corpse when the zombie dies instead.
[*] [b]No tier display[/b]: to see a bag's tier, check which "Reinforce Bag" recipe accepts it. Only the next tier's recipe does.
[*] [b]Old bags show capacity 49 or 48[/b]: the engine caps a bag's capacity at 50 minus its own weight. BUP bags weighed 0.1, so the save stored 49. After the switch they use the vanilla weight, so an ALICE pack (2.0) shows 48. Items packed up to 49 are all still there and can be taken out, and converting the bag brings it back to 49.
[*] [b]Changed upgrade sandbox options can cost Hydration Pack water[/b]: converting an old BUP Hydration Pack, or upgrading or reverting a reinforced one, recalculates its water capacity from the current sandbox values, and water that no longer fits is lost. For example, with a highest tier of 0 the 5 L in an old BUP Hydration Pack drops to 3 L, and a full capacity below 100 also cuts water. Unchanged sandbox values never lose water; with only the highest tier changed to 1 or 2, converting an old BUP Hydration Pack still keeps 5 L.
[*] [b]Renamed fanny packs swapped earlier[/b]: if a renamed fanny pack was swapped front or back on version 0.1.0 or without this mod, its first upgrade or conversion resets the name to the default. The vanilla swap copies the name but does not mark it as custom, so this mod cannot tell it is yours. Rename it again before upgrading to keep the name.
[*] [b]One reinforced satchel at a time[/b]: two reinforced satchels both need the second satchel slot.
[*] [b]Satchel straps cross over[/b]: on female characters, a reinforced cloth satchel worn with a vanilla satchel has straps that clip into each other a little where they cross on the chest and back when seen up close.
[*] [b]Attachment slots on four families only[/b]: only the reinforced Military Backpacks, Duffel Bags, Hiking and Big Hiking Backpacks have them; the originals and other bags do not.
[*] [b]Blowtorch with B42PackMule[/b]: when B42PackMule's sandbox option "Attach: Welding Torch" is on (the default), the blowtorch uses its back slot instead and does not fit this mod's side strap.
[*] [b]Adding mid-save[/b]: containers that already generated loot do not get new bags.
[*] [b]Server ran without the original mod[/b]: old bags loaded during that time are already deleted and this mod cannot restore them. See step 4 of "Server Switch".
[*] [b]EFT compatibility version not supported[/b]: saves from Bag Upgrade Plus EFT compatibility (3781184534) are not taken over.
[*] [b]Old bags cannot be upgraded[/b]: use "Convert Old Plus Bag to Reinforced" to go straight to the highest tier.
[*] [b]Bags of the same shape upgrade into the same bag[/b]: for example, the Police Duffel Bag and the sports duffel bag both become Duffel Bag (Reinforced), with the sounds and tags of that shape's base bag and the Bag category. Reverting brings back the original bag.
[/list]
=== ❓ FAQ
[h3]Can I run this together with Bag Upgrade Plus?[/h3]
[list]
[*] No. With both enabled, the console shows a warning and BUP overrides the definitions of some old bags.
[*] When coming from BUP, swap them in a single restart as described in "Server Switch".
[/list]
[h3]What happens if I remove this mod?[/h3]
[list]
[*] This mod's bags, every reinforced bag (that is, every upgraded bag) and the old bags it took over disappear, along with everything inside them.
[*] To keep upgraded vanilla bags, use "Revert Reinforced Bag to Original" before removing the mod. The upgraded capacity and Encumbrance Reduction are stored on the bag and stay after removal (capped by the original bag's weight, for example 48 for a Military Backpack).
[*] Items hung on a reinforced bag's attachment slots are not lost: they were in your inventory all along, and after removal they stay there, just no longer attached.
[/list]
[h3]Why did my bag's name change after upgrading?[/h3]
[list]
[*] The first upgrade turns the bag into its reinforced version: it weighs 0.1, does not slow you down and gets "(Reinforced)" after its name. Vanilla bags also switch to the S.T.A.R.S. or Umbrella look, with the theme in front of the name. Its contents and your custom name stay.
[*] To go back to the original, use "Revert Reinforced Bag to Original"; the tier stays and you get back the original bag and color.
[/list]
[h3]Can I change the theme of a reinforced bag?[/h3]
[list]
[*] Yes: use "Switch Reinforced Bag Theme (S.T.A.R.S./Umbrella)" on a reinforced vanilla bag. The Needle is kept, it takes 1 Thread and no skill. Tier, contents, liquid and your custom name stay.
[/list]
[h3]What about bags I upgraded in version 0.1.0?[/h3]
[list]
[*] Use "Convert Upgraded Bag to Reinforced": no materials used, the tier stays, and capacity and Encumbrance Reduction are recalculated with the new rules (tier 3 becomes 49/100).
[*] Tier 1 and 2 bags can also just use the next upgrade recipe, which turns them reinforced as well.
[/list]
[h3]Can I wear two satchels at once?[/h3]
[list]
[*] Yes: one normal satchel plus one reinforced satchel, which sits on the other side. Two reinforced satchels do not work together.
[/list]
[h3]A full bag on the floor won't let me pick it up. What now?[/h3]
[list]
[*] Right-click it and choose "Wear (with contents)". You put it on directly without emptying it first.
[/list]
[h3]Does it work in single player?[/h3]
[list]
[*] Yes, it works in single player and multiplayer.
[*] Single-player saves can take over BUP bags too; see the single-player part of "Server Switch".
[/list]
[h3]Can I add it to an existing save?[/h3]
[list]
[*] Yes. New bags only appear in containers that have not generated loot yet; zombie drops work right away. Vanilla bags you already have can be upgraded at any time.
[/list]
[h3]Why can't I find any new bags?[/h3]
[list]
[*] Try army surplus store backpack shelves or police station storage and gun storage first; they are the easiest places.
[*] Containers you already opened are not refilled. If the server sets the "Loot and zombie drop multiplier" to 0, the bags never appear.
[/list]
[h3]Does upgrading empty the bag?[/h3]
[list]
[*] No, items can stay inside. The bag only needs to be taken off.
[/list]
[h3]Why can't I put anything into my old bag?[/h3]
[list]
[*] The sandbox option "Lock old Bag Upgrade Plus bags" is on by default. Convert it into a reinforced bag (items can stay inside), or ask the server owner to turn the option off.
[/list]
[h3]Is there any difference between S.T.A.R.S. and Umbrella?[/h3]
[list]
[*] Stats are identical; only the colors and loot locations differ. When sewing, you pick the one you want.
[/list]
