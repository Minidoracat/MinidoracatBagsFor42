description=[h2]📦 Requirements[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] Works in single player and multiplayer; in multiplayer the server must enable this mod
description=[*] [b]Add/remove mid-save:[/b] safe either way; removing it deletes this mod's bags, the reinforced bags, the old bags it took over, and everything inside them

description=[h2]🚀 Quick Start[/h2]
description=[olist]
description=[*] Find S.T.A.R.S. bags at police stations and Umbrella bags at military and medical sites
description=[*] Take a bag off and upgrade it from the Tailoring category of the crafting menu; the first upgrade makes it reinforced
description=[*] Convert an old Bag Upgrade Plus bag into the reinforced version of the same bag from the crafting menu
description=[*] Server owners coming from Bag Upgrade Plus: follow the switch steps in the full guide
description=[/olist]
description=
description=[h2]✨ Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Seven custom bags[/b]: from hip pouch to expedition pack, in S.T.A.R.S. and Umbrella colors
description=[*] [b]Reinforced upgrades[/b]: vanilla bags and this mod's bags get bigger, lighter and stop slowing you down; vanilla bags switch to S.T.A.R.S. or Umbrella colors
description=[*] [b]Second satchel[/b]: reinforced satchels sit on the other side, so you can wear one more
description=[*] [b]Attachment slots[/b]: reinforced military backpacks, duffel bags and hiking backpacks hold bottles, plushies and tools
description=[*] [b]Wear full bags[/b]: put on a full bag straight off the floor with everything still inside
description=[*] [b]Loot and drops[/b]: found at police, military and medical sites, and dropped by matching zombies
description=[*] [b]Bag Upgrade Plus takeover[/b]: old bags stay in your save; switch steps are in the full guide
description=[*] [b]Server settings[/b]: upgrade size, highest tier and spawn rate are all sandbox options
description=[/list]
