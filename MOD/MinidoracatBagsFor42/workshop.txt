description=[list]
description=[*] Works in single player and multiplayer; in multiplayer the server must enable this mod
description=[*] [b]Add/remove mid-save:[/b] safe either way; removing it deletes the new bags, the old bags it took over, and everything inside them

description=[h2]🚀 Quick Start[/h2]
description=[olist]
description=[*] Find S.T.A.R.S. bags at police stations and Umbrella bags at military and medical sites
description=[*] Take a bag off and upgrade it from the Tailoring category of the crafting menu, up to three times
description=[*] Empty an old Bag Upgrade Plus bag and convert it into a new one from the crafting menu
description=[*] Server owners coming from Bag Upgrade Plus: follow the switch steps in the full guide
description=[/olist]
description=
description=[h2]✨ Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Custom looks[/b]: all seven bags come in S.T.A.R.S. navy and Umbrella khaki
description=[*] [b]From hip pouch to expedition pack[/b]: hip pouches, ammo bandolier, satchel, backpacks and chest rig
description=[*] [b]Dedicated storage[/b]: the medic pouch holds medical supplies, the bandolier holds rounds and reloads faster
description=[*] [b]Bag upgrades[/b]: vanilla bags and this mod's bags both upgrade to more capacity and less weight
description=[*] [b]Loot and drops[/b]: found at police, military and medical sites, and dropped by matching zombies
description=[*] [b]Bag Upgrade Plus takeover[/b]: old bags stay in your save; switch steps are in the full guide
description=[*] [b]Server settings[/b]: upgrade size, limits and spawn rate are all sandbox options
description=[/list]
